FFXIV METEOR MODULE EMBLEM

File: ff14-meteor.svg
Transparent SVG, viewBox="0 0 100 100", fill="currentColor".
The hollow center is an evenodd transparent cutout. Each palette supplies its
own accent color when rendering the same local asset.

This is a hand-authored reference rendering, not an official vector or exact
logo file. Its familiar hollow ring and asymmetric flames were recreated with
reference to Square Enix's official product photography:
